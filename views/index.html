🎯 Recruit Targets

A 35-recruit target board built from your current roster, depth chart, and NIL situation — not just a generic top-recruits list. Every recruit is scored on positional need (factoring in graduating Juniors/Seniors), fit vs. your current players at that position, NIL cost-effectiveness, how interested they actually are in your program, and home-state/pipeline ties. Fullbacks, Kickers, and Punters are excluded from the board entirely.
